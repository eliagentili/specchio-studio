  end of stream, reconnection and reset.

  simulated end of stream; duration observed in the last test: 0.457 seconds.

  OCR, transparency and rotation.
• H.264 video export with background, frame, rotation, flip and audio;
  three actual presets with verified dimensions, trim, duration and audio.

Signature verification was local ad hoc verification, not Developer ID or
notarization. No physical iPhone/iPad, real USB connection, Local Network
consent or macOS capture picker was tested in that round. New assets and
documents were to be included in final packages for rechecking before release.

Repeatable commands:
./test.sh
./build.sh
codesign --verify --deep --strict "Specchio Studio.app"

Fixtures are local and synthetic. Result files are generated under
.build/test-results and are not included in source packages. An environment
that blocks macOS codec services can cause failures unrelated to the app’s
codec implementations: run tests in a normal macOS Terminal.

was finalized by stopping Screen Mirroring on the phone, without pressing

app returned to its waiting state.

Personal recordings are not included in the source archive. These

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Specchio Studio turns iPhone and iPad captures into product demos,
creator assets and clearer bug reports on your Mac.
A project by Elia Gentili. Free experimental preview for Apple Silicon
Macs running macOS 14 or later, with English and Italian app interfaces.

GITHUB DESCRIPTION
Native Mac studio for iPhone/iPad captures, demos, creator exports and bug
reports. Local OCR and opaque masks. By Elia Gentili. Apple Silicon/macOS 14+.

AUTHOR PRESENTATION
I created Specchio Studio to bring together the work after a screenshot:
preparing a demo, covering selected details, exporting several formats
and explaining a bug with the right attachments.
The project combines SwiftUI, native capture, media pipelines and local
OCR in a Mac app. The experimental prerelease is free, with corresponding
source and checksums. Final hardware checks, notarization, provenance and
rights review remain open and are disclosed in the release notes.

LANGUAGE POLICY
The website, GitHub presentation and public marketing materials use
English. The app offers English and Italian separately, with English as
the default and an optional system-language setting. Earlier website
links under /it/ redirect to English; no parallel Italian site is kept.
Version 1.3.1 remains an earlier release with an Italian app interface.

STATUS AND USE

Describe the build as experimental, intended for testing and feedback,
ad hoc signed, without Developer ID or notarization. Gatekeeper rejects
the build in local assessment; macOS can block a downloaded copy.

The upstream FairPlay uncertainty also remains unresolved.

KEEP THESE CLAIMS PRECISE
Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14+, English and Italian app interfaces.
Local media processing and OCR; no app account required.
Fixed video masks, without motion tracking.
OCR and privacy suggestions require review and are not exhaustive.
Original recordings, audio and manual report text are not automatically
redacted. Including an unmasked original in a report requires opt-in.
Do not claim perfection, universal compatibility, market exclusivity
or the complete absence of legal risk.
